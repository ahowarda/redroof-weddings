How It Works

Three Lodging Options.
One Property.

Red Roof Farmhouse has grown into three lodging options on one property, each designed for a different size of gathering. The Lodge sleeps 18, the Farmhouse sleeps 27, and the Village brings it all together for groups of up to 31.

Woven throughout all three spaces you'll find family heirlooms collected over generations. Little pieces of history tucked into every corner that give this place a soul you simply can't manufacture.

Whether you're planning a wedding weekend, a family reunion, a corporate retreat, or time away with the people who matter most, there's a version of Red Roof Farmhouse made for you.
