The Property

100 Years of Idaho Farmland

What started as a working Idaho farmhouse in 1921 has grown into something far more than anyone could have planned. Over the past century, this land has been home to harvests, families, celebrations, and quiet evenings under open Idaho skies.

Today, Red Roof Farmhouse has been lovingly restored into three distinct lodging spaces, the Lodge, the Farmhouse, and the Village, each one designed to welcome a different size of gathering while preserving the soul of the original property.

The land hasn't changed. The views of Idaho farmland stretching to the horizon, the old-growth trees strung with lights, the gathering grounds where ceremonies take place, these have been here far longer than any renovation. We simply gave them a reason to keep welcoming people.

A place like this doesn't get built. It gets inherited, restored, and passed forward. That's what Red Roof Farmhouse is.

100 Years of History

The Heirlooms That Live Here

Woven throughout all three spaces, you'll find family heirlooms we've collected over generations. Little pieces of history tucked into every corner. They aren't decor. They're memories that belong to this land, and now they belong to the experience of staying here.

Every guest notices something different. A piece of furniture, a photograph, a tool that belonged to someone who worked this same soil a century ago. That's the soul of Red Roof Farmhouse. You can't build it. You can only inherit it.

  • Antique furniture restored and placed throughout the spaces
  • Vintage photographs and artwork from Idaho's early settlers
  • Hand-crafted pieces passed down through the family
  • Original farmhouse architectural details preserved throughout
  • Collected pieces from over a century of Idaho life
