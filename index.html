Our Story

More Than a Property

What started as a 100-year-old Idaho farmhouse has grown into something we never could have planned. JoJo and Jeff have lovingly restored this historic property into a one-of-a-kind retreat that serves as a gathering place for families, couples, and groups from across the Treasure Valley and beyond.

Woven throughout all three spaces, you'll find family heirlooms collected over generations. Little pieces of history tucked into every corner that give this place a soul you simply can't manufacture.

This is not just a rental. It's a place that remembers the people who have gathered here, and looks forward to the ones still to come.
