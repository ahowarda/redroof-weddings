About the Venue

A Place That Feels Like Home

Tucked into the farmlands of Nampa, Red Roof Farmhouse is a 100-year-old property lovingly restored by JoJo and Jeff. With multiple buildings, outdoor ceremony spaces, a labyrinth garden, treehouse, and zip line, your wedding here feels personal, not packaged.

We host small, intentional weddings for couples who want something real over something formal. No strangers walking through. No cookie-cutter ballrooms. Just your people, open Idaho skies, and a setting that photographs beautifully from every angle.

We live on the property and personally care about your day from the first inquiry to the last dance.
